## Membership

To officially join, go to the Ideal Logic site (https://apps.ideal-logic.com/osusee), log in
with an OSU ONID, search for "Computer Hardware Club", and click join.

There are no member dues. Anyone affiliated with OSU is eligible, from first-time builders to
experienced sysadmins.

## About the club

The Computer Hardware Club at Oregon State University is a student-run organization that helps
students build résumés and get real, valuable experience. Members are encouraged to pick up
ambitious projects of any subject matter and the club supports them along the way. If it
involves electricity, the club is game.

All skill levels and backgrounds are welcome. The club runs hands-on workshops, project
collaborations, and regular meetings to bridge classroom learning and real implementation.

## Club server "Peter"

The club owns a Dell PowerEdge R620 that Peter is named after: 2x Intel Xeon E5-2670 (16 cores
total), 32GB DDR3 ECC RAM, dual 750W PSUs.

Members do not currently have access to the server. The club does not have dedicated campus
rack space yet, so it cannot run full-time. Do not promise access or uptime for it.
